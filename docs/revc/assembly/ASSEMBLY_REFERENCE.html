Top view

All 90 LEDs: physical cathode to the left. The red pad and bar identify the PCB cathode, regardless of supplier pin numbering. D91 is polarized: Nexperia PESD5Z6.0,115 pin 1 cathode to VBUS_5V; pin 2 anode to GND. Match its cathode mark to the red pad 1. U3 is TPS7A2433DBVR.
